# People, equipment, creatures and buildings

Working content briefs, 2026-09-29. Sheet numbers refer to the
[concept pack](README.md). Names and abilities are proposals.

## People

Outfits suggest occupations and backgrounds. Players learn and combine skills;
equipment expresses their choices. The early region should support exploration,
combat, technical work and social problem solving on the same journeys.

| Sheet | Role and silhouette | Skills and place in the world | Distinctive production details |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Independent surveyor: long split ochre coat, slim pack, folded probe above one shoulder | Surveys deposits and failed stations, reads tracks, finds routes; a plausible new arrival with portable expertise | Split coat clears legs, probe folds for interiors, one large cuff instead of many small gadgets |
| 02 | Borough freight runner: cropped red jacket, broad cargo harness, knee brace | Knows the pass roads, handles cargo contracts and local introductions; later can manage a hired route | Standard latches, reinforced lifting points and replaceable knee brace; civilian load-bearing gear |
| 03 | Continuity field medic: teal tunic and ivory satchel | Treats injuries, samples water, recognizes ecological changes; unlocks sampling and medical work | Medical cassette shares its shell family with other cases; cuff and exposed neck implant show ordinary augmentation |
| 04 | Pelagic infrastructure warden: heavy charcoal armor and pale shoulder block | Guards essential plant, escorts service crews, enforces access rules; a contact, employer or opponent depending on the dispute | Armor over work clothes, safe weapon carry, modular helmet and restrained indicators |
| 05 | Independent fabricator: apron, navy coveralls, obvious prosthetic forearm | Repairs equipment, teaches schematics, commissions materials and introduces local suppliers | Broad practical tools, apron pockets, prosthetic joints with clear motion; an older resident with expertise |

The initial named residents can be a fabricator who wants reliable filter
stock, a runner negotiating safe route access and a medic investigating a
changing marsh. Their jobs overlap at the same damaged station. Each can help
the player in a different way and disagree over the source of replacement water.
Keep names and biographies open until the opening mission is authored.

## Equipment

| Sheet | Working item | Adventure use | Crafting and visual relationship |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Survey receiver | Gives bearing and a rough deposit reading; a placed probe verifies yield and quality | Improved coils extend useful survey range; physical screen keeps it readable as a held object |
| 02 | Implant interface cuff | Hosts a field utility such as survey filtering or drone control | One utility slot in the first proposal; fitting requires a clinic, keeping medicine and town visits relevant |
| 03 | Pulse carbine | Compact ranged option for dangerous routes | Battery cartridge, receiver and cooling sleeve are recognizable service parts; balance and damage are undecided |
| 04 | Induction repair tool | Opens service panels and repairs approved equipment | Repairs consume a part appropriate to the fault; the clamp head can share a component family with factory tooling |
| 05 | Medical cassette | Field stabilization and sample handling | Crafted seals and sterile inserts replenish it; medicine is obtained through the clinic or medic |
| 06 | Ceramic filter cartridge | Restores a pump, brine skid or monitoring station | First useful factory output; quality affects service life, with a standard minimum that always works |
| 07 | Cargo canister | Carries samples, parts and contract goods | The small personal case shares the freight pod's shell and latch language; gameplay inventory capacity remains a separate decision |
| 08 | Scout drone | Visits a marked probe or retrieves a nearby sample within sight | Upgradeable battery and sensor module; initial behavior can be one explicit command at a time |

Start with a few understandable properties: capacity, range, protection and
service life. Specialized variants can favor one property at a cost elsewhere.
Better tools broaden jobs and reduce friction. Basic equipment must remain
useful throughout the basin, including for players who buy supplies.

Crafted equipment retains its material lot and schematic identity in its
inspection view. An NPC can recognize a locally made filter or request a
particular tolerance without demanding the player become a full manufacturer.

## Creatures

Native life should change how a player reads a place. Imported trees and native
animals coexist; successful terraforming can still displace the original
ecology. These four animals propose a common lineage, with six limbs and
paired sensory slits. Resolve anatomy in orthographic drawings before modeling.

| Sheet | Creature and approximate adult scale | Habitat and behavior | Encounter or industry connection |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Reedback: 1.8 m shoulder, roughly 3.5 m long | Grazes reeds and engineered groundcover; family groups cross wet lowland trails; notices vibration before sight | Repeated pump vibration alters its route. A culvert or different extraction schedule can preserve passage; approaching calves provokes a warning stance |
| 02 | Latchmaw: 0.8 m shoulder, roughly 2 m long | Ambushes small animals at woodland edges; tracks exposed food rather than attacking every traveler | A leaking biological cargo seal attracts it. Fixing the seal and using the maintained road can resolve the route problem |
| 03 | Siltbell: 0.4 m high, roughly 0.9 m long | Scavenges damp debris in drainage channels; retreats under roots; clusters reveal nutrient runoff | A medic uses its distribution as evidence. Blocking or poisoning the drainage creates a visible local consequence |
| 04 | Glasswing: 2.5 m span | Glides over marsh thermals, perches on disused sensor masts, follows emerging insects | Its nesting pattern guides a player to an old station and warns of wind exposure; relocation can use a replacement perch |

Show threat through behavior: stop, orient, display, approach, then attack if
provoked or hunting. Idle feeding, retreat and travel are as important as
combat. Wildlife problems should admit observation, rerouting, repairs or
dialogue where those make sense. A hunting contract can exist, but routine
industrial play should not require exterminating the landscape.

Variants use age, body mass, shield color and scarring. Distinct species require
distinct silhouettes and behaviors. Only close animals need detailed animations;
population and distance budgets must be measured with the game's streaming.

## Buildings

Reuse [Port Serein's four construction generations](../port-serein.md#3-four-generations).
An institution's occupants and fittings can distinguish buildings made in the
same generation. These concept proportions do not override the city's scale
table or doorway dimensions.

| Sheet | Family and use | Reusable recipe parts | Variation and approach |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Landing service module: canteen, repair office, clinic or survey hut | Rounded composite envelope, thick oval frames, inset hatch, roof service fittings, awning | Panel replacements, tank position, porch and roof additions; entrance faces the path or shared court |
| 02 | Borough workshop-house: independent home and livelihood | Reused shells, concrete plinth, shutter bay, external stair, balcony, corrugated lean-to, cistern | Offset upper module, paint ages, additions from different owners; freight goes to the workshop and pedestrians to the stair |
| 03 | Pelagic annex: pumping, fabrication and access control | Chamfered piers, slit openings, recessed entrance, broad loading shutter, pipe gallery | Program changes the roof plant and service side; large structural rhythm and a controlled approach communicate institutional power |
| 04 | Continuity station: samples, field medicine and monitoring | Landing shell plus sample-room module, covered porch, isolated wash fittings and sensor mast | Station instruments change by habitat; a separate wash/sample route protects the public entrance |

A workshop needs an outdoor loading apron, a place for full and empty pods,
access to power, a clear pedestrian path and a reason for its residents to stay.
Keep those spaces in the blockout. Put additions where a previous owner needed
them. Local slope, road access, drainage and sight lines should determine the
building's orientation and foundations.

Industrial wear concentrates at docking rails, filters, grips and runoff paths.
Household care appears in repaired panels, awnings and planted containers.
Pelagic equipment can be maintained while the company defers investment in
housing; the contrast can tell a story before a conversation starts.
