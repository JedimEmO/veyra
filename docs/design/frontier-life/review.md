# Concept review and handoff limits

2026-09-29. All selected images were visually inspected. This is a first art
direction pass. The drawings establish families and relationships; they do not
certify rigging, scale, collision, material budgets or finished asset topology.

| Board | Useful result | Remaining modeling decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| People | Five distinct working silhouettes, shared practical clothing and visible personal repairs | Coat and apron deformation, cuff attachments and prosthetic joint ranges need front/side/back sheets; simplify small pouches |
| Equipment | Strong silhouettes for a receiver, cuff, repair clamp, filter and cargo case; readable material families | Tool grips and connector dimensions need a scale drawing; the carbine's generated markings are incidental |
| Creatures v2 | Grazer's shorter neck and divided feeding face give it a stranger identity; predator's middle legs are clearer | Far-side joints and glider wing attachment remain ambiguous in perspective; six-limb gait and folded-wing pose need dedicated studies |
| Buildings | Landing and Borough share shells; Pelagic has heavy structural mass; Institute visibly adapts Landing construction | Human doorway and ramp sizes must follow the existing style table; painted material wear must simplify in recipes |
| Production and freight | Machines have distinct functions and consistent detachable cargo; the crawler and pod share visible latches | Draw docking clearances, hopper removal path, crawler turning circle and machine moving parts before mesh authoring |
| Workyard | Road arrival, residents, storage, fabrication and a processor horizon combine into an inhabited location | The extractor reads taller and closer to the building than its 4 m brief; give it a separate pad in the blockout, and keep pedestrian/loading access clear |

The creature revision is the selected fauna reference. The background animal in
the workyard scene has a more familiar four-legged appearance and does not
define the reedback's anatomy. The scene also emphasizes a two-storey workshop
and adds bus-stop activity; these are composition proposals. Its brine and power
units are less distinct than the equipment sheet, which is their reference.

Generated imagery adds small serials and symbols even where prompts request
numbers only. Treat these as disposable decoration. Faction insignia, medical
markings and diegetic labels require authored artwork and text when implemented.

These new images use written setting and style constraints. They are not edits
of current game screenshots, except the focused revision of the first creature
sheet. Their foliage, hills, sky and water are not renderer targets and should
not redirect concurrent terrain or weather work.

For the first blockout, validate a pod arriving by crawler, unloading beside the
fabricator and being carried through a workshop opening. Validate the surveyor's
probe pack through the same doorway. One shared scale test will expose more
useful problems than separately detailing each asset.
